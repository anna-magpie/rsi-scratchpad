Each slider is a conditional probability: the chance this link holds given that every earlier link held. Read that way, multiplying them is exact. Most of the classic "conjunctive fallacy" objection comes from people setting each link as if it were independent of the others.

The defaults are placeholders, not anyone's estimate. Drag them to your own numbers. The URL updates as you go, so you can share a configuration.

Bars show the running product: the probability that links 1 through n all hold.

Things to try

  • Set every link to 0.7. The product is 8%, which feels low for a chain where each link is "likely". Then ask whether 0.7 was really your conditional credence. Given links 1 to 3, isn't link 4 more likely?
  • Find the one link where moving from your value to 1.0 changes the product the most. That is where evidence is most valuable to you.
