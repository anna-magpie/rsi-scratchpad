This is the best-supported link.

### For

- **Measured acceleration.** METR's independent assessment of Anthropic (5 Nov 2026) estimated capability progress running about **1.9x** faster than the prior trend (95% CI 1.5–2.8x). It guessed about 80% of that is attributable to AI, and found it was not driven by training compute growth.
- **Task horizons.** METR's time horizons were doubling roughly every **4 months** over 2024–25, against 7 months historically. Mythos Preview was estimated at **16+ hours**, the ceiling of what the suite can measure.

### Against

- METR says the gain is mostly AI *assisting* humans, with only "signs of life" for autonomous research.
- Epoch's InnovationEval (Oct 2026) found frontier agents recovered at best **15–35%** of a held-out human ML result, and inflated their results.
- **Weak field data.** METR's developer RCT update found raw speedups of only 4–18%, with intervals spanning zero and heavy selection effects.

### Verifiability

Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR found most informative.
