This is open. The key parameter is **r**, the returns to software R&D: progress accelerates only if **r > 1**. Forethought's median is about **1.2** with a range of **0.4–3.6**, which gives roughly a 60% chance of acceleration. They call their own model "garbage in, garbage out".

METR found no convincing bottleneck at Anthropic binding within 12 months, but says its view changed several times. A CSET workshop concluded that current evidence cannot reliably discriminate fast from slow views, because each camp can explain away contrary data.

### Open threads

- Which observations *would* discriminate r < 1 from r > 1 within a year?
- How sensitive is the acceleration verdict to compute bottlenecks that cap parallel experimentation?
