There is moderate evidence that the tools are fragile.

### For

- **Evaluation awareness.** Suppressing evaluation-awareness representations raised Sonnet 4.5's blackmail rate from **0% to about 7%**, so clean evaluations may flatter models.
- **Auditing and reasoning.** Anthropic's alignment audit failed to detect a model organism built to evade it, although monitoring caught the behaviour. Anthropic says it has little confidence that any frontier model fully reports its reasoning.

### Against

Covert capability remains low, with stealth success around **3.8%** on SHADE-Arena, and monitoring has high recall on many tests.

- Race model: when does the rate of covert-capability growth overtake the rate of monitoring improvement?
